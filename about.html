Our Story

Savanna Brew was born from a simple dream — to bring people together over great coffee. Our journey started in Nairobi, Kenya, with a passion for quality coffee, warm hospitality and a desire to create a cosy space where everyone feels at home.

Meet the founder: Amina Otieno, a coffee lover, entrepreneur and proud Kenyan, who believes in the power of good coffee to inspire, connect and uplift.
